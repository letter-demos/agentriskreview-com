Show New Carriers the Record You Have Already Earned

For independent agents and brokers: a Producer Risk Profile gives carriers and MGAs an independent, third-party document of your licensing history, loss trends, and book of business.

Who it is for

Independent agents and individual brokers who are applying for a new carrier appointment, onboarding with an MGA, or expanding into specialty lines, and who need a credibility document that does not come from themselves.

The problem, in your words

“I had no way to show a new carrier that my book was clean and my compliance history was solid.”

“I had no idea how to present my compliance record to an MGA that did not know me.”

Carriers want your record documented, organized, and presented by someone other than the agent making the application. A clean record is no longer enough on its own if you cannot show it in a form carriers trust.

How Agent Risk Review helps

The Producer Risk Profile is an individual-level evaluation written by our independent review team and formatted for carrier and MGA submission.

  • Personal licensing and disciplinary history review
  • Loss ratio trend summary based on provided carrier data
  • Portfolio concentration and diversification analysis
  • Attestation letter suitable for carrier or MGA submission
  • Turnaround of five to seven business days

Our evaluations are never influenced by carriers, MGAs, or any party with a financial interest in the outcome. That independence is what makes the document worth submitting.
