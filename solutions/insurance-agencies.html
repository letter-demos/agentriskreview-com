Keep Your Agency's Compliance Record Carrier-Ready, All Year

For small to mid-size agencies: an independent compliance baseline, then ongoing monitoring of licenses, E&O coverage, and regulatory changes, so problems surface before a carrier finds them.

Who it is for

Agency owners and principals running small to mid-size independent agencies who need compliance documentation they can hand to carriers, and ongoing oversight they do not have the staff to run in-house.

The problem, in your words

“I used to find out about regulatory changes way too late.”

“Keeping up with license renewals and E&O continuity was becoming a real liability.”

Regulatory requirements shift constantly, and a brief lapse in errors and omissions coverage can raise serious red flags during carrier review. A point-in-time check is not enough when the rules keep moving.

How Agent Risk Review helps

We start with an Agency Compliance Baseline Report, a foundational review of your licensing records, E&O coverage history, binding authority adherence, and internal controls against current state and carrier standards. Then we keep watching.

  • Verification of active licensure across all operating states
  • Review of errors and omissions coverage continuity and limits
  • Monthly compliance status checks across all active state licenses
  • Alerts for renewal deadlines and regulatory updates
  • Quarterly written risk summary delivered to the agency principal
  • Dedicated account reviewer assigned to your agency

Every report reflects the actual situation of your agency, not a template with names swapped in, and is written in plain language that agents, carriers, and regulators can all understand.
