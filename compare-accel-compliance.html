Agent Risk Review vs. ACCEL Compliance

An independent evaluation you can hand to carriers, or a compliance team and software that run your filings and licenses? For agents, brokers and agencies, these are two different jobs. Here is how each firm describes what it does, and how to decide which one you need.

Based on each firm's own pages, read in November 2026. See sources.

The short answer

Agent Risk Review

An independent evaluator. We review your licensing records, E&O coverage history, binding authority adherence and internal controls, and give you written findings formatted for carrier submission. We do not file, maintain licenses or provide compliance software.

ACCEL Compliance

A compliance services vendor. In its own words, "a team of compliance professionals dedicated to helping insurance agents and brokers satisfy their regulatory requirements efficiently," supported by its GreenLight software, which is also offered on its own.

If you need documentation a carrier will take seriously

This is the work Agent Risk Review is built for: an outside, written account of your record that you can hand to a carrier. Our evaluations are never influenced by carriers, MGAs or any party with a financial interest in the outcome, and they are carried out by reviewers with backgrounds in state insurance regulation, carrier underwriting and agency management.

  • Agency Compliance Baseline Report: verification of active licensure across all operating states, a review of E&O continuity and limits, and an assessment of binding authority usage against your carrier agreements, delivered as a written findings report formatted for carrier submission.
  • Producer Risk Profile: an individual-level review of licensing and disciplinary history, a loss ratio trend summary from the carrier data you provide, portfolio concentration analysis and an attestation letter suitable for carrier or MGA submission.
  • Carrier Submission Package: the full Baseline Report with a carrier-facing executive summary, a documentation checklist aligned to the target carrier's requirements and one round of revisions based on carrier feedback.

Our reports document where you stand. They do not promise an appointment or a particular underwriting outcome; carriers make those decisions.

If you need someone to run the compliance work

ACCEL Compliance describes hands-on compliance operations. "From licensing to surplus lines tax filings to producer onboarding to Secretary of State obligations, the ACCEL team works every day with regulators in all US jurisdictions."

  • Services: License Maintenance, Surplus Lines Tax Filings, Producer Onboarding & Management and Managed Annual Reports.
  • GreenLight Compliance Management System: software built by ACCEL's team, including a License Management System that keeps licensing details and documents in one place with automated reminders, a Surplus Lines Tax Filing System and a Producer Onboarding System. ACCEL offers these tools to agents and brokers who prefer to manage their own compliance activities. The platform is cloud-based and interfaces with NIPR and state insurance departments.
  • BlueBack Compliance Library: a regulatory reference center with state-by-state requirements, maintained by ACCEL's compliance team.
  • Pricing model: affordable subscription access combined with activity-based fees, for clients ranging from Top 25 agencies to smaller start-ups.

What each one does not do

Agent Risk Review is an evaluator, not an operating vendor. We do not file surplus lines taxes, maintain licenses, onboard producers or provide compliance software. Our monitoring subscription tracks license renewals, E&O gaps, regulatory changes and carrier compliance requirements and tells you what needs attention; the work of acting on it stays with your agency or the vendor you use.

ACCEL Compliance covers a broad range of operational compliance work across US jurisdictions, with software to match. The pages we read do not describe an independent evaluation or a carrier-ready report as a deliverable. If that is what you need, ask ACCEL directly whether it offers one.

Which to choose, or using both

  • Choose Agent Risk Review when you are applying for a new carrier appointment, expanding into specialty lines or answering a carrier's questions, and you need a third-party document that shows your licensing, E&O and binding authority record.
  • Choose ACCEL Compliance when the need is getting compliance work done: keeping licenses current, making surplus lines tax filings, onboarding producers and meeting Secretary of State obligations, through its team or its software.
  • Use both when one firm runs the day-to-day compliance work and you want an outside party to evaluate the result. The roles are different: one does the work, the other gives carriers an independent view of where your agency stands.
