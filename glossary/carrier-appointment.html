Carrier Appointment

A carrier appointment is the formal authorization an insurance company gives a licensed producer to sell and service its policies.

A carrier appointment is the formal authorization an insurance company grants a licensed producer or agency to solicit, sell and service its policies. Holding a state license makes an agent eligible to sell insurance; an appointment is what connects that agent to a specific carrier's products.

Before granting an appointment, carriers review the applicant's licensing status, disciplinary history, errors and omissions coverage and, for established agencies, past production and loss experience. In many states the carrier must also file the appointment with the state insurance department, and appointments can be terminated by either side.

Because carriers are reviewing agent compliance records more closely, gaps in documentation are a common reason appointments are delayed or declined. A complete, independently verified file gives the carrier fewer open questions to resolve.
