Glossary

Plain definitions of the compliance, licensing and carrier terms insurance agents and agencies run into most.

Binding Authority

Binding authority is the power a carrier grants an agent to put coverage into effect on the carrier's behalf, within set limits.

Carrier Appointment

A carrier appointment is the formal authorization an insurance company gives a licensed producer to sell and service its policies.

Errors and Omissions (E&O) Insurance

Errors and omissions insurance is professional liability coverage that protects agents and agencies against claims of mistakes or negligence in their work.

Loss Ratio

Loss ratio is the share of premium a carrier pays out in claims, and one of the measures carriers use to judge the quality of an agent's book.

Producer License

A producer license is the state-issued license that allows an individual or agency to sell, solicit or negotiate insurance in that state.
