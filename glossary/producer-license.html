Producer License

A producer license is the state-issued license that allows an individual or agency to sell, solicit or negotiate insurance in that state.

A producer license is the license a state insurance department issues to an individual or business entity, allowing it to sell, solicit or negotiate insurance in that state. Licenses are issued by line of authority, such as property and casualty or life and health.

An agent licensed in their home state holds a resident license and needs a non-resident license in each additional state where they do business. Each license carries its own renewal date, continuing education requirements and fees, which is why multi-state compliance can become hard to track.

A license is a prerequisite for a carrier appointment, and carriers verify that licenses are active for every state and line an agent intends to write.
