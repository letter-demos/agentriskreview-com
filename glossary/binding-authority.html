Glossary

Binding Authority

Binding authority is the power a carrier grants an agent to put coverage into effect on the carrier's behalf, within set limits.

Binding authority is the power a carrier grants an agent or agency to put coverage into effect on the carrier's behalf, without waiting for the carrier to approve each risk individually. The scope is spelled out in the agency agreement or a separate binding authority agreement.

That agreement typically limits which lines, classes of business, territories and policy limits the agent may bind. Binding outside those limits exposes the carrier to risks it did not agree to underwrite, and it is one of the faster ways for an agency to lose an appointment or have its authority restricted.

Carriers reviewing an agency often ask how binding authority is used and controlled in practice. Clear internal procedures and records of adherence to each agreement help answer that question.
