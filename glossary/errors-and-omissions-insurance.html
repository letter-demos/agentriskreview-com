Errors and Omissions (E&O) Insurance

Errors and omissions insurance is professional liability coverage that protects agents and agencies against claims of mistakes or negligence in their work.

Errors and omissions (E&O) insurance is professional liability coverage for insurance agents, brokers and agencies. It responds to claims that a producer made a mistake, gave inadequate advice or failed to do something they should have, such as placing the wrong coverage or missing a renewal.

Most carriers require proof of E&O coverage, at minimum limits they set, before granting or renewing an appointment. Many also look at continuity: a lapse in coverage, even a short one, can prompt additional questions during carrier review.

Agents should keep declarations pages, effective and expiration dates and renewal confirmations for every E&O policy they have held, so coverage history can be shown rather than simply stated.
